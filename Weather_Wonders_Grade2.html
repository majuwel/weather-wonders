What Is Weather?

Weather is what the air and the sky are like outside, right now.
It can change in one day!

Sunny Weather

Sunny Days
  • The Sun gives us light and heat.
  • Plants and flowers need sunshine to grow.
  • Stay safe: wear a hat, sunscreen, drink water and find shade.
Let's talk! What do you like to do on a sunny day?

Rainy Weather

Rainy Days
  • Rain falls from clouds.
  • Rain gives plants and animals a drink.
  • Take an umbrella and wear a raincoat!
Close your eyes. Listen. Can you hear the pitter-patter?

Cloudy Weather

Cumulus
Puffy and white, like cotton wool.
Stratus
A flat grey blanket that covers the sky.
Cirrus
Thin, wispy feathers high up.
Storm Cloud
Tall and dark. Brings rain and thunder.

1

2

Hot and Cold Weather

Hot Days
It is warm and sunny. About 30°C!
We wear:
👕 🩳 🧢 🕶️ 🩴
T-shirt, shorts, sun hat, sunglasses, sandals
Cold Days
Brrr! It is chilly. About 5°C!
We wear:
🧥 🧣 🧤 🧦 🥾
Coat, scarf, gloves, warm socks, boots

Weather Song

Verse 1
Sun is shining warm and bright,
Clouds are floating, soft and white,
Rain falls pitter-patter down,
Puddles splash all through the town.

Verse 2
Wind goes whoosh and whirl and blow,
Thunder rumbles, soft then low,
Hot days: sandals, shorts and sun,
Cold days: coats, then out we run!
Wind goes whoosh and whirl and blow,
Thunder rumbles, soft then low.
♪
Actions: Sun = big arm circle  •  Clouds = float hands  •  Rain = wiggle fingers  •  Puddles = jump  •  Wind = sway  •  Thunder = stamp!
